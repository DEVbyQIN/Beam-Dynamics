\section{九项\texorpdfstring{$\alpha$}{alpha}系数与极限}\label{app:alpha}
式~\eqref{eq:alpha}的参数如下：
\begin{center}\begin{tabular}{lrrr}\toprule
系数 & $C_\alpha$ & $(p_x,p_y,p_z)$ & 球极限 $k_x=k_y=1$ \\ \midrule
$\alpha_x$ & $k_x^2$ & $(3,1,1)$ & $1/3$ \\
$\alpha_y$ & $k_y^2$ & $(1,3,1)$ & $1/3$ \\
$\alpha_z$ & $k_xk_y$ & $(1,1,3)$ & $1/3$ \\
$\alpha_{xx}$ & $3k_x^4/2$ & $(5,1,1)$ & $0.3$ \\
$\alpha_{yy}$ & $3k_y^4/2$ & $(1,5,1)$ & $0.3$ \\
$\alpha_{zz}$ & $3k_xk_y$ & $(1,1,5)$ & $0.6$ \\
$\alpha_{xy}$ & $4k_x^2k_y^2$ & $(3,3,1)$ & $0.8$ \\
$\alpha_{xz}$ & $k_x^2$ & $(3,1,3)$ & $0.2$ \\
$\alpha_{yz}$ & $k_y^2$ & $(1,3,3)$ & $0.2$ \\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
同时检查x/y交换及$(k_y/k_x)\alpha_x+(k_x/k_y)\alpha_y+\alpha_z=1$。独立参考为
\begin{equation}
 \alpha_x=\frac{k_x^2}{3}R_D(k_y^2,1,k_x^2),\quad
 \alpha_y=\frac{k_y^2}{3}R_D(k_x^2,1,k_y^2),\quad
 \alpha_z=\frac{k_xk_y}{3}R_D(k_x^2,k_y^2,1).
\end{equation}
这些核对系数，不证明高斯闭合对所有真实分布准确。
